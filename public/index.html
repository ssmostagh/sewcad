Quick-Start Studio Guide

From body measurements to 1:1 printable sewing patterns in 4 steps
1
Pick Block & Your Size

Choose a women's sloper, dress, skirt, trouser, or .STL cover at top. In 1. Size & Fit, select your US size preset (XS–2X) or enter custom measurements in in / cm.

2
Dial Fit & Sculpt 2D Curves

Drag sliders (or click any amber number badge to type an exact measurement). Click any 2D pattern edge to curve necklines, add darts/notches, or unfold on fold right from the top canvas bar.

3
Drape & Check Fit in 3D

Garments re-drape automatically when you release sliders. Press Space (Drape 3D) for live physics, Ctrl+Click cloth to pin to the form, or toggle Fit Strain Map.

4
Print 1:1 at Home

Click Print 1:1 PDF (Letter or A4 pages with corner alignment diamonds & a 2.00 × 2.00 in / 50 × 50 mm test square — print at 100% Actual Size).
